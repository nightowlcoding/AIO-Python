ℹ CSV Format Requirements

Wide spreadsheet format:

  • Row 1: starting at column D, each cell is an inventory date

📦 Understanding Package Sizes & Inventory Units

Important: All inventory quantities represent full units based on the Package Size:

  • 60 EA → Inventory of 1 = 1 unit of 60 items
  • 4/5 LB → Inventory of 1 = 1 unit (4 pieces at 5 lbs each)
  • 12/6/3.5 OZ → Inventory of 1 = 1 unit (12 cases with 6 items per case at 3.5 oz each)

When entering inventory, always count complete units as shown in the Package Size column.
